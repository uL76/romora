Lumbini Natural Park, Berastagi

Home to North Sumatra’s iconic golden pagoda, Lumbini blends serene Buddhist architecture, landscaped gardens, and cool mountain air—an easy cultural escape from Medan.

Lumbini Natural Park

Golden Pagoda & Tranquil Gardens

Discover a Peaceful Mountain Sanctuary

Nestled in Berastagi’s highlands, Lumbini Natural Park features a striking golden pagoda inspired by Myanmar’s Shwedagon, surrounded by manicured gardens, a sky bridge, and quiet meditation halls. It’s a favorite spot for photography, reflection, and refreshing walks in the cool mountain breeze—perfect before or after a visit to Berastagi’s fruit markets and volcano viewpoints.

Top Attractions at Lumbini

Architecture, serenity, and scenic walks

The Golden Pagoda

Admire intricate Buddhist details and golden spires—one of Sumatra’s most photogenic landmarks.

Sky Bridge & Garden Walks

Stroll along the elevated walkway for panoramic views over flower beds and the pagoda complex.

Meditation Hall & Prayer Wheels

Step inside for a moment of calm; learn basic etiquette and spin the prayer wheels mindfully.

Essential Travel Tips

Make the most of your Lumbini visit

Best Time to Visit

Dry season (May–September) has clearer skies; weekends and holidays can be busier with local visitors.

Getting There

About 2–3 hours by road from Medan (traffic dependent). Private car or arranged transport recommended.

Etiquette & Dress

Modest clothing is appreciated around the pagoda and halls. Be quiet and respectful inside prayer areas.

Gallery

Golden architecture and mountain garden scenes

Pair Lumbini with Berastagi’s viewpoints and markets for a relaxing highland day trip from Medan.

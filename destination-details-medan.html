Medan, Indonesia

Cultural Crossroads of North Sumatra

Discover the Energy of Medan

As the capital of North Sumatra, Medan is a dynamic city blending colonial heritage, vibrant street food, and diverse ethnic cultures. It serves as the perfect gateway to explore Lake Toba, Berastagi, and other stunning destinations in the region.

Top Attractions in Medan

Experience the unique blend of history, culture, and flavor

Maimun Palace

A stunning example of Malay-Islamic architecture, home to the legacy of the Deli Sultanate.

Great Mosque of Medan

One of Indonesia’s most beautiful mosques, blending Moorish, Indian, and European influences.

Medan Culinary Streets

Explore flavorful dishes like Bihun Bebek, Soto Medan, and Durian Pancake on foot or with a guide.

Essential Travel Tips

Things to know before visiting Medan

Best Time to Visit

May to September offers the best weather with fewer rain showers.

Weather & Climate

Tropical climate with temperatures ranging between 24–32°C (75–89°F).

Currency

The local currency is Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Credit cards are widely accepted in urban areas.

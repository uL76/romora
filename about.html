Our Story

From the Island, to the World

Romora Tour Travel was born to fulfill the need for comfortable, flexible, and memorable journeys across North Sumatra. From daily car rentals to full tour packages with drivers and guides, we are committed to delivering the best experience to every customer.

We believe every journey should be tailored to personal desires. That's why initial communication is essential to customize your route, accommodation, and destination preferences.

Our Vision

"To become the leading travel and transportation provider from Lake Toba to the entire archipelago."

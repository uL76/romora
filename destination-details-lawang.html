Bukit Lawang

Wild Orangutans & Jungle Trails

Sumatra’s Classic Jungle Base

Set along the emerald Bohorok River, Bukit Lawang is a relaxed rainforest village on the edge of Gunung Leuser National Park—one of the last strongholds of the critically endangered Sumatran orangutan. Come for guided treks, river tubing, and slow evenings by the water.

Top Things to Do in Bukit Lawang

Wildlife encounters, caves, and river fun

Orangutan Trekking

Half-day to multi-day guided hikes to spot wild orangutans, Thomas leaf monkeys, gibbons, and hornbills.

Bat Cave (Gua Kelelawar)

Easy walk through limestone chambers and stalactites—bring a torch and sturdy shoes.

River Tubing

Float back to the village after a jungle trek—fun rapids, helmets and life vests provided.

Jungle Waterfalls

Short hikes to hidden cascades for a jungle dip—great add-on to a morning trek.

Day Trip to Tangkahan

Soak in hot springs, cross jungle suspension bridges, and enjoy a quieter riverside setting.

Village Walks

Meet local producers, see cocoa and rubber plantations, and try traditional coffee.

Essential Travel Tips

Plan your jungle escape

Best Time to Visit

Wildlife is year-round; drier months (May–September) mean firmer trails. Expect afternoon showers anytime.

Getting There

About 3.5–4.5 hours by road from Medan (120 km). Private cars and shared minibuses depart daily.

Conservation & Safety

Always use licensed guides. Keep 10 m from wildlife; no feeding or touching. Wear good shoes; leech socks help in rainy season.

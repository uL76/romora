Nias Island

Surf, Stone Jumping & Island Life

Discover Indonesia’s Legendary Wave & Living Heritage

Beyond its iconic right-hand break at Sorake–Lagundri, Nias offers centuries-old culture in hilltop villages like Bawömataluo, famous for the ritual stone jumping (Fahombo). Spend your days surfing glassy walls, snorkeling coral gardens around the Hinako Islands, or tracing megaliths and traditional wooden houses (Omo Hada) across the interior. Base yourself in Gunungsitoli for museums and markets before venturing south to the surf coast.

Top Attractions in Nias

From perfect waves to living traditions

Lagundri & Sorake Bay

Consistent right-handers with dry-season swells—bucket-list waves for intermediate to expert surfers.

Bawömataluo Village

See traditional houses and the famous Fahombo stone-jumping ritual in a UNESCO-listed cultural landscape.

Hinako Islands

Hop across tiny isles for snorkeling, freediving, and powder-white beaches far from the crowds.

Essential Travel Tips

Plan your Nias adventure like a pro

Best Time to Visit

Dry season (May–September) for surfing consistency; shoulder months offer fewer crowds and mellow swells.

Getting There

Fly to Binaka Airport (Gunungsitoli) via Medan, then continue by road to Sorake/Lagundri or cultural villages.

Safety & Etiquette

Respect line-ups and local customs; in villages, ask before photos and dress modestly during ceremonies.

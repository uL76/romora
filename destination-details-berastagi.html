Berastagi

Highland Charm & Volcanic Adventures

North Sumatra’s Highland Gateway

Nestled at 1,300 meters above sea level, Berastagi offers crisp mountain air, colorful fruit markets, and access to two active volcanoes: Mount Sibayak and Mount Sinabung. Whether you come for sunrise hikes, hot springs, or simply a relaxing escape from Medan’s heat, Berastagi is the perfect highland retreat.

Top Attractions in Berastagi

Hikes, culture, and fresh mountain vibes

Mount Sibayak

A relatively easy hike to a steaming crater with sunrise views over the Karo Highlands.

Berastagi Fruit Market

Sample passion fruit, oranges, and strawberries at the bustling central market.

Lumbini Golden Pagoda

An impressive replica of Myanmar’s Shwedagon Pagoda set against lush hills.

Gundaling Hill

Panoramic viewpoints of both Sibayak and Sinabung volcanoes, especially at sunrise and sunset.

Hot Springs

Relax in steaming pools at the base of Mount Sibayak after a rewarding hike.

Mount Sinabung

A dramatic active volcano—best admired from safe viewpoints around the region.

Essential Travel Tips

Plan your Berastagi getaway

Best Time to Visit

Year-round cool weather; dry months (May–September) are best for clear volcano hikes.

Getting There

Two hours by road from Medan (90 km). Private cars, minibuses, and tours run daily.

Where to Stay

Berastagi town offers budget stays and mountain-view hotels. Hot spring resorts sit near Sibayak’s base.

Tangkahan, North Sumatra

Known as Sumatra’s “Hidden Paradise,” Tangkahan is an eco-tourism haven where dense rainforest meets crystal-clear rivers and gentle elephants. Perfect for those seeking jungle trekking, elephant bathing, and serene river adventures.

Tangkahan Eco-Tourism

Where Elephants and Rainforest Meet

Discover the Hidden Paradise

Nestled in the Gunung Leuser National Park, Tangkahan offers a unique combination of pristine rainforest, adventurous rivers, and eco-conscious tourism. Visitors can trek through lush jungle, cross suspension bridges, soak in natural hot springs, and interact with Sumatran elephants cared for by the local community.

Must-Visit Attractions

Experience the highlights of Tangkahan

Elephant Bathing

Join the local mahouts in bathing and feeding Sumatran elephants in the clear river waters.

River Tubing

Float down the crystal-clear Buluh River on an inner tube for a fun and refreshing adventure.

Jungle Trekking

Explore lush rainforest trails, discover hidden waterfalls, and spot wildlife in their natural habitat.

Essential Travel Information

What to know before visiting Tangkahan

Best Time to Visit

Dry season (May–September) for easier trekking and river activities. Rainy season offers greener scenery but slippery trails.

Getting There

Located about 3–4 hours by road from Medan. Private car or arranged transport is recommended.

Local Tips

Bring insect repellent, trekking shoes, and swimwear for river activities. Respect conservation rules when with elephants.

Currency & Facilities

Transactions are mostly cash (IDR). Limited phone signal, so prepare offline maps and essentials.
